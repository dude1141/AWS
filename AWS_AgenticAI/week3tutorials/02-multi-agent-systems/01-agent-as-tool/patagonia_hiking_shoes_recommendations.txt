KEY CONSIDERATIONS FOR PATAGONIA
=================================================================

✓ WATERPROOFING: Absolutely essential
  - Look for Gore-Tex or equivalent membrane
  - Patagonia has frequent rain and wet conditions

✓ TRACTION: Aggressive lugs required
  - Must handle mud, wet rock, and loose scree
  - Vibram or Contagrip outsoles recommended

✓ ANKLE SUPPORT: Highly recommended
  - Essential for multi-day packs (30+ lbs)
  - Prevents injuries on uneven terrain

✓ BREAK-IN TIME: Critical!
  - Start wearing boots 4-6 weeks before trip
  - Do several training hikes with full pack
  - Prevent blisters on your actual trek

✓ GAITERS: Bring compatible gaiters
  - Essential for river crossings
  - Protects from mud and debris
  - Keeps feet drier longer

=================================================================
ADDITIONAL RECOMMENDATIONS
=================================================================

SIZING TIPS:
- Buy boots half size larger than normal shoes
- Account for thick hiking socks
- Toes should not touch front when going downhill
- Try on boots in afternoon when feet are swollen

SOCK RECOMMENDATIONS:
- Merino wool hiking socks
- Bring 3-4 pairs minimum
- Consider liner socks to prevent blisters

PRO TIPS:
- Consider bringing BOTH boots (for technical terrain/heavy 
  pack days) AND trail runners (for easier sections) on 
  longer expeditions
- Test footwear on similar terrain before your trip
- Apply moleskin at first sign of hot spots
- Dry boots thoroughly each night

TERRAIN YOU'LL ENCOUNTER:
- Rocky mountain trails
- Loose scree and talus
- Mud and wet grass
- Stream/river crossings
- Occasional snow patches (depending on season)
- Steep ascents and descents
